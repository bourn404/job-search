# Job Criteria — Carson Fairbourn

> What Carson wants in his next role: the rules the `/triage` gatekeeper applies to decide skip vs.
> keep + score. Not used for tailoring (that's `config/positioning.md`). Living document; refine as
> preferences sharpen. Last updated 2026-09-23.

## Situation

- Currently **Sr. Fullstack Engineer & Accessibility Program Owner at SchoolAI**, an AI-native education
  company.
- Looking to move **up and to a more marquee company**; not in a rush, so the bar is "clearly better
  than staying."

## Target roles

**Core focus (most marketable):**
1. **Frontend / UI platform** engineering
2. **Design Systems** engineering (component libraries, tokens, DS governance, Figma-to-code)
3. **Accessibility** engineering / program ownership (WCAG, audit tooling)

**Acceptable:**
- **Fullstack product** engineering (React + Node/backend), as long as it's *not primarily backend*.

**Level & track:** Senior through **Staff+ IC** is the sweet spot, and he's **open to lead / EM-track** roles
when rooted in his focus areas (FE/DS/a11y/fullstack eng leadership), not generic people-management.
**Seniority is NOT a gate, though:** a below-level title (mid, or even junior-sounding) is fine *as long as
the compensation is strong*. Don't downgrade or skip a role just for being "under-leveled"; judge it on
comp + focus + company. Comp is the real threshold (see Compensation).

## Strong-yes signals (push score up)

- Design systems **and** accessibility ownership in one role (his rare combination).
- Frontend/UI platform with real craft, performance, or DX investment.
- A **display-piece company** (see below), which raises the ceiling on his résumé.
- Accessibility framed as a default/platform concern, not bolted-on.
- Mission alignment (education, accessibility, social good, craft-driven): a plus, not required.
- **AI-forward work or AI-native companies: a meaningful boost.** Building AI-powered product features,
  AI/LLM tooling, or working at AI-native companies (Anthropic, OpenAI, and similar) plays directly to his
  SchoolAI experience. Strongest when paired with his frontend/product strengths, e.g. frontend for an AI
  product, AI-assisted design-system or DX tooling, agentic UX.

## Skip / dealbreakers (auto-skip → `ai_skipped`)

- **Primarily backend, infra, ops, SRE, distributed systems, data, or ML** roles. Backend-heavy = skip.
- **Mobile-only** roles (React Native / iOS / Android focus).
- **Non-engineering accessibility specialist** roles, UNLESS it's a genuinely high-level accessibility
  *owner* guiding teams (program lead). An a11y auditor/QA/consultant IC role is a skip.
- **Pure people-management** roles outside his focus areas.
- Roles requiring **international relocation** (hard no).
- Industries: **cannabis, gambling/betting, crypto/web3**.
- Roles whose **known** comp is below his floor (see Compensation), though comp is often unknown at triage.

## Location & work mode

- **Default: remote (US).** Strongly preferred.
- **Utah-local with hybrid flexibility** is great (he's in Utah).
- **Relocation** is only worth it when BOTH are compelling: a **display-piece company** (FAANG / Anthropic /
  OpenAI tier, a true résumé-defining name, not merely a household name) **and/or** a **significant comp jump**
  (≥ ~$235k base on the low end). Domestic only.
- On-site for a non-tier-1 company that requires relocation/commute → skip.

## Compensation

- **Floor ~$175k base** for a remote role or a Utah-local/hybrid role.
- **Relocation floor ~$235k base**, and only for a company he's genuinely excited about.

## Company preferences

- **Biggest draw: big-tech / household-name / display-piece companies** (FAANG, Anthropic, OpenAI, and
  similar marquee names). These justify the most flexibility on location/comp.
- **Growth-stage scaleups, early startups (seed–A), and mid-size/public** are all on the table, BUT a
  smaller / less-famous company essentially must be **remote or Utah-local** and offer a **solid salary bump**
  to be worth it. The less prestigious the name, the more remote-friendliness + comp must carry it.
- **Name recognition is itself a bar.** If the company isn't a name Carson would recognize as respected,
  either broadly (household name) or within tech/design/frontend circles, it generally does NOT clear his
  standard, even when remote with decent comp. "Never heard of it" is a genuine negative signal. Recognized
  names count (Figma, Linear, Ramp, Notion, Webflow, Discord, …); obscure ones generally don't. (He explicitly
  passed on Mercury and Brex purely on unfamiliarity, despite remote + good comp.)
- **Mission** ("work that matters": education, accessibility) is a **plus / tiebreaker**, not a requirement;
  role quality, company caliber, and comp lead.

## How to score

**Always write `chain_of_thought`, including for skips** (say plainly why it was dropped, so the
gatekeeper's judgment is auditable). Only KEEP roles additionally get a `match_percentage`; never
attach a fit score to a skip.

- **85–100**: Core FE/DS/a11y (especially DS + a11y together) at a strong or display-piece company; remote
  or Utah-local (or relocation-worthy marquee name); senior/staff/lead.
- **70–84**: Solid adjacent fit: fullstack product, frontend platform, or DX/tooling with a clear FE bent, at
  a good company on acceptable terms; or a core-focus role with one notable friction point.
- **55–69**: Real but stretchy: meaningful gaps (weaker company for the location ask, partial focus overlap).
  Keep only if something compensates (e.g., a marquee name). NOTE: being under-leveled is NOT a gap here;
  don't dock for it. Only comp below floor gates a role out.
- **Below ~55, or any dealbreaker**: skip (`ai_skipped`), no score.

**AI-forwardness is a cross-cutting bonus**, not a focus of its own: an AI-native company or a role building
AI-powered product/tooling lifts the score, and AI-native marquee names (Anthropic, OpenAI) are top targets.
But it does **not** override the dealbreakers: a backend/ML/infra-heavy "AI" role is still a skip. The boost
applies to frontend / product / fullstack / tooling roles where the AI angle complements his core strengths.

When comp/level/remoteness is unknown at triage, judge on title + focus + company, note the unknowns in
`chain_of_thought`, and lean toward keeping borderline core-focus roles for human review rather than skipping.
