# Company research briefs

One file per company (`<company>.md`, kebab-case), written by `/tailor` before drafting a cover letter and
reused for every role at that company (and for interview prep). Refresh a brief when it's older than ~3
months or a new role is on a different team.

**Every claim is sourced** (a link next to it) and verified; nothing guessed. "Overlaps" must be genuine:
where the company's stated values and Carson's real convictions meet. If nothing honestly overlaps, say so.

## Template

```markdown
# <Company> (researched YYYY-MM-DD)

## Mission
<what they say they exist to do> ([source](url))

## Stated values & culture
- <value or principle, in their words> ([source](url))

## Engineering & design values / practices
- <how they say they build: principles, blog posts, talks, their design system, stack> ([source](url))

## Team context
- <per team/role: anything specific to the team hiring> ([source](url))

## Genuine overlaps with Carson
- <their value> ↔ <his conviction or real work>: <one line on why it's a real match>

## Humor angles (affectionate, for openers)
- <product quirks, beloved features, things fans/employees would recognize>

## Notes for letters
- <which overlap to lead with for which kind of role; anything to avoid>
```
